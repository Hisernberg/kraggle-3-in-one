**Exact metric** (`ch/metric.py` matches the official one). Each annotator record `<annotator>-<stem>` is scored
against the same predictions:
- A (gt, pred) cell with IoU > 0.5 is a TP, an unmatched pred is an FP, an unmatched gt is an FN.
- Counts are pooled over all records: `PQ = ΣIoU(TP) / (TP + 0.5 FP + 0.5 FN)`.

**Metric quirks:**
- **Prediction charge:**
  - Each prediction is charged once per record. Train has 411 single-, 145 double- and 151 triple-annotated
    files (1,154 records).
  - The PQ-optimal rule keeps candidate c iff its annotator-expected matched IoU u(c) > τ = PQ*/2 ≈ 0.22–0.26
    (Dinkelbach). One global τ is optimal.
- **Human agreement:** human vs human PQ 0.340, SQ 0.634.
- **OOF → LB drop:** about −0.05 to −0.06 for every honest pipeline:
