## 1. Rules and metric

`S_total = 0.35·S_state + 0.30·S_queue + 0.15·S_physics + 0.20·S_ODME`, per panel, averaged over 2 directions then
5 families. A missing/invalid task, or a task with any required row absent, scores 0. Public = March (5 incidents),
private = April (7 incidents).

- **Task 1:** per regime (20/30/50 % masked) `0.54·max(0,1−RMSE_v/25) + 0.46·max(0,1−RMSE_q,lane/600)`, mean over
  regimes, eligible target cells only. Offline: any released cell of the split may be used (transductive is legal).
- **Task 2:** space-time IoU on eligible horizon cells; mean of 5 windows per (panel, condition) → panel → family;
  8 panels. Queue = underlying speed ≤ 0.6·v_f. Onset truth only at T+30 (#742350). Strict ≤ T data, no post-buffer
  data (#742068). #745700 (is slot T allowed?) is unanswered.
- **Task 3:** `(S_FD + 2·S_LWR)/3`; `S_LWR = 1 − Σ|ΔN − rhs|/Σ|rhs|`, `N = (q/max(v,1))·L`; only target cells carry
  error. S_FD forfeited if > 20 % of submitted flows < 50 vph.
- **Task 4:** `0.45 S_od + 0.25 S_link + 0.15 S_dev + 0.15 S_attr`. Our L2 projection scores 0.9938 (ceiling +0.0012).

Public-LB sensitivity: one queue window = 0.30/80 = 0.00375 of the total per unit IoU, readable at ±0.003 with 5
decimals. Teams with 80–95 submissions can hill-climb March queue windows; that does not transfer to April. Our own
Task 2 deltas below ±0.0015 are noise (40-window LB SD 0.005–0.009 on one condition).
