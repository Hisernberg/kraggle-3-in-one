### Research block after S1 (00:10-01:15 UTC): correction of the S1 reading
A Laplace error model fitted to S1 (E|e+d| = |m+d| + b exp(-|m+d|/b), with the public FL MAE assumed 4.5-7.5 mm)
shows that most of S1's loss is curvature (rows whose sign flips), not imbalance:

| assumed FL MAE | median error m | predicted LB at x0.975 (-2.14 mm) | optimal shift | LB gain at the optimum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.5 mm | +0.48 mm | +0.0069 | -0.48 mm | -0.0007 |
| 5.5 mm | +0.79 mm | +0.0027 | -0.79 mm | -0.0015 |
| 6.5 mm | +1.11 mm | -0.0003 | -1.11 mm | -0.0025 |
| 7.5 mm | +1.43 mm | -0.0027 | -1.43 mm | -0.0036 |

So my first reading ("60-64 % over-predicted") overstated the bias. The FL level is only slightly high
(median error about +0.5 to +1.4 mm); a -2.14 mm step could overshoot and lose. **S2 is revised to x0.988
(-1.0 mm mean)**, which is close to the optimum under every assumed MAE.
- Revised prediction: -0.0005 to -0.0030 (at the noise floor; global change, so it transfers if real).
