## Research block (00:06-00:35 UTC): non-square pixels in the 1200x800 test images
- New topic 748126 ("Questions on the stretched 1200x800 images, pennation angle and the split", no replies yet).
- 1200x800 images are two families: juniper_right_* (Siemens Juniper 12L3, 91 rows) and left_ticks_800 (Telemed
  Echo Wave UI, 90 rows) = 59 % of the test set. Our scale uses the vertical depth ticks only.
- left_ticks_800 shows lateral 10 mm ticks along the bottom edge: 134.5 px spacing (IMG_00147, IMG_00100), depth
  ticks 148.1 px per 10 mm -> horizontal / vertical px per mm R = 0.908 (non-square pixels, 10 %).
  In pixel geometry a true angle reads tan(theta_px) = tan(theta) / R (~ +1.3 deg at 14 deg) and a true length reads
  L * sqrt(R^2 cos^2 + sin^2) (~ -9 %). MT (vertical) is unaffected. Juniper shows no lateral scale (not testable).
- Whether this matters depends on the label convention: raters measuring the provided files in FIJI with the depth
  scale share the distortion (then no correction; my prior ~75 %); labels from the original exports would be in true
  geometry. LB history cannot separate it (the d11 long-FL clips mix three families).
- New `blend_v2.py --aniso FAMILY R TARGETS` converts final values of a family from pixel to true geometry.
